## About the Justice of the Peace
The Justice of the Peace (JP) in Texas is the judicial official closest to the people. Duties include:
- Presiding over Class C misdemeanor cases
- Handling civil cases up to $20,000
- Small claims and landlord/tenant disputes (evictions)
- Issuing search and arrest warrants
- Setting bail
- Conducting preliminary hearings
- Performing marriage ceremonies
- Conducting death inquests (coroner duties)
- Handling truancy and juvenile matters
- Managing court docket and administrative duties
